# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Model developers, transcription-tool builders, and technical reviewers who need to inspect transcript-cleanup evidence, compare model behavior, and review JSONL records without parsing raw artifacts.

## Product Purpose

Provide a static, inspectable research workspace for a fixed transcript-cleanup benchmark and its supporting datasets. The primary task is close evidence review: move quickly between cases, compare source and target text, and understand model quality in context.

## Brand Personality

Direct, rigorous, and readable. The interface should feel like a confident research tool used at a desk, with a bright white working surface and strong editorial hierarchy.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark-mode-driven presentation, dashboard-card clutter, decorative gradients, generic AI styling, and visuals that make metrics more prominent than the underlying transcripts.

## Design Principles

- Put the source, reference, and model response at the center of each evidence task.
- Make movement through records fast with visible controls and keyboard shortcuts.
- Preserve the distinction between measured benchmark data and unavailable provider data.
- Use bold structure and restrained color to guide attention without distracting from text.
- Keep all reading, filtering, and local-file review accessible with standard browser controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion-safe styles, and a layout that remains usable on narrow screens. Arrow shortcuts must not interfere with typing in form fields.
