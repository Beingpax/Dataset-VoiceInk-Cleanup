# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Model developers, transcription-tool builders, and technical reviewers comparing small transcript-cleanup models on a shared validation sample. They need to inspect aggregate quality, runtime behavior, prompt differences, and every individual output without reading raw JSON.

## Product Purpose

Create a reproducible benchmark and editorial showcase for one hundred cases across two labeled sources: fifty VoiceInk validation cases (the original twenty random cases plus thirty longer-input additions) and fifty supplied curated input/reference pairs. Compare three local cleanup configurations and a hosted GPT-5.6 Sol low-reasoning baseline while preserving the evidence needed to reproduce and audit every claim.

## Brand Personality

Measured, editorial, and candid. The interface should feel publishable and composed while keeping methodology, limitations, and raw evidence close to every result.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI gradients, leaderboard-only claims, decorative glass panels, and visual treatments that hide uncertainty or conflate two differently constructed datasets.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the comparison, then make the evidence one interaction away.
- Distinguish measured facts, derived scores, and unavailable provider metrics.
- Treat prompts and runtime settings as first-class benchmark inputs.
- Make small-sample uncertainty visible instead of implying general superiority.
- Preserve raw transcripts and line breaks in every detailed comparison.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No separate accessibility program is in scope. Use ordinary semantic HTML, readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, and reduced-motion-safe transitions as baseline implementation quality.
